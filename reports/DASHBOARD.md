# Dashboard data guide

Where every number in the project lives, what the files contain, and what a dashboard must not show
without context. The full write-up is [REPORT.md](REPORT.md); this file is the practical map for
building views on top of it.

Everything here is **read-only input**. The CSVs and figures are regenerated by the commands in
REPORT.md section 11. Please do not edit them by hand or retrain from the dashboard.

## 1. What the project found, in five lines

- 17 bearings (6 learning, 11 test), run to failure, one vibration snapshot every 10 s.
- 11 models were compared: a constant "predict the average" floor, Ridge and Random Forest
  (baselines), and six deep models (MLP, LSTM, GRU, 1D-CNN, CNN-LSTM, CNN-LSTM + attention).
- **No deep model clearly beats the Random Forest.** On the validation split everything ties at
  about 63–65 min MAE. On the official PHM score only the 1D-CNN is ahead, with caveats (section 5).
- The cause is in the data: most bearings fail abruptly with no warning in vibration, and 6 of the
  11 test bearings look healthy at their cut point yet have 10–126 min of life left.
- Used as a maintenance alarm, the best models give a useful warning (2–60 min ahead) for about
  5 of 11 bearings, typically only a few minutes before failure.

Parsing notes:
- `outcome` is one of `useful`, `too late`, `early`, `missed`. `lead_min` and `warn_at_h` are empty
  when the outcome is `missed`.
- `*_learning_split_piecewise.csv` (without `_per_seed`) has a **two-row header** (metric, then
  mean/std). Use `pd.read_csv(path, header=[0, 1], index_col=[0, 1])`, or just use the `_per_seed`
  files and aggregate yourself.
- In `benchmark_cutpoint_*` the deep models have 3 seeds and the others 1, so average over `seed`
  before comparing.
- Test bearings are `Bearing1_3`..`1_7`, `2_3`..`2_7` and `3_3`. Learning bearings are `1_1`, `1_2`,
  `2_1`, `2_2`, `3_1`, `3_2`. The validation split trains on the first four learning bearings and
  tests on `3_1` and `3_2`.

## 5. Rules for showing the numbers honestly

1. **Always show the `constant_mean` row next to any PHM score.** It scores 0.107 / 0.156. A model
   that does not clearly beat it has learned nothing usable. Most do not.
2. **Rank models on `phm_score_5min_mean`, not on `phm_score`.** The score at the official cut is one
   snapshot per bearing and mostly luck: `rf_row` scores 0.257 there but 0.161 averaged over the
   5 minutes before it, level with the constant's 0.156.
3. **Do not read the 1D-CNN's 0.201 as "the best model".** It also has the worst full-curve MAE
   (132 min) and predicts early more often, and the PHM score punishes late predictions much harder
   than early ones. It ties every other model on the validation split and does not beat the
   Random Forest on maintenance warnings.
4. **Bearing1_4 is doubtful.** Its recorded RUL at the cut (2890 s) is suspect. Offer the
   `phm_score_excl_Bearing1_4` column as a toggle.
5. **Show per-bearing results, not only pooled ones.** One bad bearing hides in a mean. Per-bearing R²
   is strongly negative for 3_1 for every model (about −5 to −7 for the deep models, down to −140 for
   some baselines) because its RUL range is short, so do not use raw R² as a headline.
6. **`full_curve_MAE_min` cannot rank models** (123–132 min for every model, constant included). It is
   dominated by early life on the long bearings.
7. **Flag `degrading_at_cut`.** 6 of 11 test bearings look healthy at the cut; no model separates
   them, so their predictions cluster around 30–60 min whatever the truth is.
8. **The MC-dropout bands are overconfident:** the ±2σ band contains the true RUL only 11–12% of the
   time (target 95%). If a band is drawn, say so on the chart.
9. **Maintenance medians are over bearings that alarmed.** `missed` bearings have no lead time, so a
   low median is not the same as good coverage. Show the outcome counts with it.
10. **Deep-model differences under about 2 min MAE are noise** (seed spread 0.2–1.4 min).

## 6. What is not available yet

- **Per-snapshot predictions are not saved.** The curves exist only as PNGs. For interactive
  actual-vs-predicted charts, a small export in `src/benchmark.py` is needed (model, seed, bearing,
  `snapshot_idx`, true and predicted RUL). Seed 0 only, every 6th snapshot (1 min) is about 1 MB; all
  seeds at full resolution is about 15 MB and should not be committed. Ask before building views
  that depend on it.
- No live model: nothing in the repo serves predictions. A dashboard would show saved results.
- The fan-motor transfer in the brief needs company data and is not built.

## 7. Regenerating

```bash
python -m src.evaluate                  # degradation + health-indicator figures
python -m src.benchmark                 # all benchmark_*.csv and benchmark_rul_* figures, ~12 min
python -m src.maintenance --models constant_mean rf_row rf_window cnn_lstm lstm gru mlp cnn1d
python -m src.models.cnn_lstm           # CNN-LSTM validation-split files
python -m src.models.learning_split     # MLP / LSTM / GRU / 1D-CNN validation-split files
```

Each run overwrites its CSVs, and `src.benchmark` and `src.maintenance` rewrite their files with only
the models you pass. Run the full list, as above, before committing.
